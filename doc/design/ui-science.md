# Science Officer UI Design

## Layout

The science officer UI is divided into the following tabs:

1. Short Range Scanner / Local Map
2. Long Range Scanner / Stellar Map
3. Ultra-Long Range Scanner / Interstellar Map

All maps are 3D and share a common interface for zooming, panning, and selecting objects of interest.

### Short Range Scanner / Local Map

The Short Range Scanner tab provides a detailed 3D map of the immediate vicinity around the ship. It symbolically displays any nearby objects such as other ships, asteroids, or space stations, along with their relative positions and distances.

Clicking on an object enables the option to scan it for more detailed information, which is displayed in a floating information panel. Additional scans can be done from certain sections of the information panel to gather more specific data, such as the status of each invidiual ship module on a target ship.

### Long Range Scanner / Stellar Map

The Long Range Scanner tab offers a broader view of the current solar system. It includes representations of planets, moons, and other celestial bodies, allowing the science officer to analyze their properties and trajectories. It also includes and registered space stations within the system.

Clicking on a celestial body or space station brings up an information panel with options to perform various scientific scans, such as atmospheric analysis, surface composition, and gravitational field measurements.

### Ultra-Long Range Scanner / Interstellar Map

The Ultra-Long Range Scanner tab provides a galactic map view, showing the positions of nearby star systems, nebulae, and other significant astronomical features. This tab is essential for plotting long-distance courses and identifying potential points of interest for exploration or scientific study.
